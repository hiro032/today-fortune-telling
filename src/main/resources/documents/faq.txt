Frequently Asked Questions - TechCorp

Q: What is the difference between AI Platform Pro and CloudServe Enterprise?
A: AI Platform Pro is specifically designed for machine learning workloads and includes ML-specific features
like model training, deployment, and monitoring. CloudServe Enterprise is a general-purpose cloud infrastructure
service that can host any type of application. Many customers use both together.

Q: How do I get started with TechCorp services?
A: You can sign up for a free trial on our website. The trial includes access to all our products for 14 days.
After the trial, you can choose the plan that best fits your needs. Our sales team is also available to help
you select the right services.

Q: What kind of support do you offer?
A: We offer three tiers of support: Basic (included with all plans), Professional ($2,000/month), and
Enterprise ($10,000/month). Higher tiers include faster response times and dedicated support teams.

Q: Is my data secure with TechCorp?
A: Yes, security is our top priority. We are SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and ISO 27001 certified.
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We also offer private cloud options for customers with strict
compliance requirements.

Q: Can I integrate TechCorp services with my existing systems?
A: Absolutely! Our products support standard APIs and protocols. We also offer pre-built integrations with
popular platforms like Salesforce, SAP, AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. Custom integrations can be developed
through our consulting services.

Q: What programming languages do you support?
A: Our AI Platform supports Python, R, Java, and Scala. Our APIs are RESTful and can be accessed from any
programming language that supports HTTP requests.

Q: Do you offer training for my team?
A: Yes, we offer both online and in-person training programs. Topics include AI/ML fundamentals, platform
usage, best practices, and advanced techniques. Training can be customized to your team's needs.

Q: What is your uptime guarantee?
A: We guarantee 99.9% uptime for all our services, backed by our SLA. Enterprise customers can get up to
99.99% uptime guarantee with additional redundancy options.

Q: How does billing work?
A: Most of our services are billed monthly based on usage. We provide detailed usage reports and cost
forecasting tools. Enterprise customers can opt for annual contracts with volume discounts.

Q: Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
A: Yes, you can cancel anytime with no cancellation fees. If you cancel mid-month, you'll have access to
services until the end of your billing period.
